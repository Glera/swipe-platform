## Decisions carried into copy-ticket

- Copy: abstract four-rule puzzle, manual marks, immediate win, sawtooth size
  bands, decreasing givens, explain-then-apply semantic hint.
- Re-design: input mapping, feedback wording, shapes, palette, motion, sound,
  tutorial copy and original datasets.
- Do not copy: animals, ZooDoku name/branding, orange ribbons, leaderboard,
  reward cadence, hearts, correctness oracle, ad boundary and exact boards.
- Still open: human novice duration, feed takeover mapping, whether an
  optional neutral hint is allowed, and first-party presentation profile
  freeze.
